Privacy Policy

IITR Campus Dashboard & Event Planner · Last updated: 28 July 2026

This app is a student events dashboard for IIT Roorkee. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Identity via Channel-i (IITR SSO): when you log in with Channel-i, we receive your name, enrolment number, IITR email, branch/year, and profile picture.
  • Your preferences: the clubs and event categories you choose to follow or hide, so we can personalize your feed.
  • Notification settings (if you enable push notifications in future): a device notification subscription so we can alert you about new events from clubs you follow.

We do not collect passwords (login is handled by Channel-i), payment information, or precise location.

How We Use Your Information

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To personalize the events/notices feed to the clubs and categories you care about.
  • To send you notifications about events (only if you opt in).

We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or publish your personal information. Content ingested from campus broadcast emails is reviewed by an admin and screened so that personal data (grades, roll-number lists, etc.) is never published to the public feed.

How We Share Information

We use a small number of service providers strictly to run the app:

  • Channel-i (IIT Roorkee): authentication.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): hosting, database, and content delivery.
  • imgbb: hosting of images you upload (e.g. event posters).

We do not share your personal data with anyone else, and never for advertising.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Amazon Web Services (Mumbai region). Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and the database is not publicly accessible. Login sessions use secure, http-only cookies.

Data Retention

We keep your account and preferences while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your data, we remove your profile and preferences from our systems.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us (below). You can also change your club/category preferences in the app at any time.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for IIT Roorkee students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
