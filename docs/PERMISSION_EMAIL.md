2. **To display the Institute's crest** in the app, at a small size, alongside a clear statement that the app is unofficial. If the Institute would prefer that the crest not be used at all, I will remove it immediately and without argument.
3. **To list the names of student clubs, societies and groups** together with their publicly announced events, so that students can find them in one place.
4. **To run the service for students of the Institute**, using Channel-i sign-in, on the terms set out below.

**Undertakings I am giving in advance:**

- Student data is limited to what Channel-i already provides — name, enrolment number, institute email, branch and year, profile picture — and is stored on servers **in Mumbai, India**. A published privacy policy sets this out, and a consent screen asks each student before anything is stored. The design follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Rules of 2025, including the requirement that accounts be limited to students aged 18 and over.

## Why this draft is shaped the way it is

It pre-commits you to four undertakings that make it **easy to say yes**: free forever, prominent "not official" notice, immediate removal on request without argument, and free handover if the institute ever wants it.

It also gives the office an **easy exit** — a short "no objection" reply is explicitly stated to be sufficient, so nobody has to draft a policy document to help you.

**Do not send it before** removing the crest is *already possible* at short notice. The offer to remove immediately has to be true.
